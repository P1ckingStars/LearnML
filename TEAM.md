## How to Get Help

### Stuck on a derivation?
1. Re-read the relevant lecture notes and referenced papers
2. Check the [Math Reference](resources/math_reference.md) for identities you might need
3. Post on the forum with your attempt so far (TA 1 will respond)
4. Come to Tuesday office hours with your work-in-progress

### Stuck on an implementation?
1. Run your gradient check — most bugs are caught here
2. Check [PyTorch Patterns](resources/pytorch_patterns.md) for idiomatic solutions
3. Post on the forum with your error traceback and a minimal reproducing example
4. Come to Wednesday office hours (TA 2)

### Stuck on your capstone?
1. Re-read the milestone spec carefully
2. Read 2–3 more papers in your area
3. Book a 1-on-1 with Prof. Arthur
4. Come to Friday office hours (TA 3) for writing/experimental design help

### Stuck on a paper you're reading?
1. Follow the [Paper Reading Guide](resources/paper_reading_guide.md)
2. Read the paper 3 times (skim, detailed, critical) before asking for help
3. Post specific questions on the forum (not "I don't understand this paper")
4. Come to Friday office hours (TA 3)

## Self-Study Adaptation

If you are working through this course independently:

- **You are the student.** The TAs are Claude instances you can consult at any time.
- **Be honest with yourself.** Attempt every derivation and implementation before asking for help. The struggle is where the learning happens.
- **Simulate the deadlines.** Give yourself 2 weeks per module. Set real calendar deadlines.
- **Do the gradient checks.** Do the ablations. Write the reports. Skipping these is how self-study courses fail.
- **Read the papers.** At least the required readings for each module. There is no substitute.
