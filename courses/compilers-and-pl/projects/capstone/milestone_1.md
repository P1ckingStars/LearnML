# Capstone Milestone 1: Problem Statement

**Due**: End of Week 5

## Deliverable

A 2-page document containing:

1. **Problem Statement**: What problem are you solving? Why does it matter?
2. **Scope**: What specifically will you build/investigate?
3. **Related Work**: 3-5 relevant papers or systems
4. **Proposed Approach**: High-level technical plan
5. **Evaluation Plan**: How will you measure success?
6. **Timeline**: Rough schedule through Week 20

## Grading (5% of capstone grade)

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Problem clarity and motivation | 30 |
| Feasibility of scope | 25 |
| Related work coverage | 20 |
| Evaluation plan quality | 25 |
